# REFINERY Public Website Proposal

## Purpose
The REFINERY website should be a primarily external-facing communications and engagement site. 

**Its job is to:**
* Clearly explain what the REFINERY is and why it exists.
* Demonstrate the breadth of infrastructure provided to robotics teams.
* Show the REFINERY's impact across Northeast Indiana and the broader FIRST community.
* Promote public programs and events.
* Publish organizational and regional news.
* Attract prospective team hosts, volunteers, partners, funders, and donors.
* Provide clear pathways into the REFINERY's separate digital systems.

> **System Boundaries:** Detailed team operations should remain within the Team Portal. Technical guides, templates, and team resources should live on the separate Documentation site. TIMS can retain its own product identity and access point.

---

## Primary Navigation
* **Primary:** Home · What We Do · Programs & Events · News · Get Involved · About
* **Prominent utility actions:** Donate · Team Portal
* **Secondary/footer links:** Documentation · TIMS · Contact

*This gives the public site six primary destinations.*

## 1. Home
The homepage should quickly answer: *What is the REFINERY, why does it exist, and why should I care?*

### Hero
> **Suggested positioning:** Shared infrastructure for stronger robotics teams.
> The REFINERY helps Northeast Indiana robotics teams start, grow, and succeed by providing funding, parts, tools, facilities, training, and a regional community.

**Primary actions:**
* Explore What We Do
* Get Involved
* Donate
*(A smaller link can direct participating teams to the Team Portal.)*

### The Problem
Briefly explain that sustainable robotics teams require much more than a robot. Teams need:
* Funding
* Parts
* Tools
* Workspace
* Technical knowledge
* Mentors
* Operational systems
* Institutional continuity

*Many schools and communities cannot independently build all of this infrastructure.*

### What the REFINERY Provides
*Use seven concise cards. Each card links to the relevant anchored section on What We Do.*
1. **Team Launch & Organizational Support:** Helping new and developing teams build the organizational foundation they need to last.
2. **Funding & Financial Infrastructure:** Helping teams raise, receive, manage, and spend funds through the REFINERY.
3. **Parts & Inventory:** Providing access to shared robot components, materials, hardware, and build-season essentials.
4. **Tools & Fabrication:** Giving teams access to specialized tools and production capabilities needed to turn designs into finished parts.
5. **Facilities & Field Access:** Providing space to build, meet, collaborate, practice, and participate in robotics activities.
6. **Training & Technical Support:** Providing students and mentors with practical instruction, resources, and troubleshooting assistance.
7. **Community & Student Experience:** Creating opportunities for students and mentors to build relationships and community beyond their individual teams.

### Beyond Northeast Indiana: Built Here. Shared With FIRST.
While the REFINERY is rooted in Northeast Indiana, many of the systems, projects, and resources we develop are intended to support the broader FIRST community. We share what we build, document what we learn, and partner with other organizations to solve challenges teams face everywhere. 

This section features four visual cards:
* **Robot in 3 Days**
* **KitBot in 3 Days**
* **TIMS**
* **Event Parts Support**

> **CTA:** See Our Contributions to the FIRST Community

### Impact Snapshot
Display a small number of strong metrics. Include **one featured story**.
* Teams supported
* Students reached
* New teams launched
* Funding provided or administered
* Grants secured for teams
* Parts distributed
* Workshops or training hours

### Latest from the REFINERY and the Region
Show a mixed feed of three to five recent stories:
* REFINERY news
* Team stories
* Regional robotics or STEAM coverage
* Partnership updates

> **CTA:** View All News

### Upcoming
Show a small number of upcoming public events or opportunities.
> **CTA:** View Programs & Events

### Final CTA
Help build the infrastructure robotics teams cannot build alone.
**Actions:** Volunteer · Partner · Donate

## 2. What We Do
This is the primary detailed explanation of the REFINERY's model. Use a compact section navigator near the top.

### Team Launch & Organizational Support
Explain how the REFINERY helps:
* Launch new teams.
* Select appropriate FIRST programs.
* Establish leadership and mentor structures.
* Develop team procedures.
* Plan budgets and purchasing.
* Onboard students and mentors.
* Prepare for competition seasons.
* Build institutional continuity.
*(Include a CTA for organizations interested in starting or hosting a team.)*

### Funding & Financial Infrastructure
Explain that the REFINERY:
* Provides funding directly to teams.
* Gives teams a platform to raise funds.
* Maintains optional team subaccounts.
* Allows teams to pursue external grants through the REFINERY.
* Administers designated and restricted funds.
* Supports team purchasing.

**Clearly explain the three spending pathways:**
1. Transfer funds to the team's school or primary financial organization.
2. Purchase items from REFINERY inventory.
3. Have the REFINERY purchase outside products selected by the team.

*(The page should describe the model publicly without exposing detailed internal financial procedures.)*

### Parts & Inventory
Explain:
* Shared robot-parts inventory.
* Electrical components.
* Hardware and fasteners.
* Materials and consumables.
* Build-season emergency availability.
* Centralized purchasing efficiencies.
*(Live inventory remains within TIMS.)*

### Tools & Fabrication
Describe capabilities such as:
* CNC routing.
* 3D printing.
* Mechanical fabrication.
* Electrical work.
* Printing and apparel production.
* Specialized tool access.
* Custom robot-part production.
*(Focus on capabilities rather than maintaining a public equipment directory.)*

### Facilities & Field Access
Explain how the physical space supports:
* Robot construction.
* Team meetings.
* Collaboration.
* Technical training.
* Practice.
* Field access.
* Scrimmages.
* Parts distribution.
* Community events.

### Training & Technical Support
Cover:
* Programming, CAD, Mechanical systems, and Electrical systems.
* CNC design and 3D printing.
* Robot tuning and Competition preparation.
* Mentor development and Technical troubleshooting.
*(Detailed guides and workshop materials link to the Documentation site.)*

### Community & Student Experience
Describe:
* Mentor Meetups.
* Cross-team collaboration.
* From Pits to Prom.
* Student social activities.
* Team Culture and Engagement efforts.
* Shared build projects.
* Outreach and demonstrations.

### Beyond Northeast Indiana: Contributions to the FIRST Community
While the REFINERY is rooted in Northeast Indiana, many of the systems, projects, and resources we develop are intended to support the broader FIRST community. We share what we build, document what we learn, and partner with other organizations to solve challenges teams face everywhere.

**Four Featured Contribution Areas:**
* **Robot in 3 Days:** The REFINERY designs and builds a robot immediately following the annual FRC game reveal, documenting the design process, prototypes, decisions, failures, and final robot for teams to learn from. *(Links to that year's Ri3D content, streams, reveal, documentation, and News posts.)*
* **KitBot in 3 Days:** A rapid, documented KitBot build focused specifically on helping developing teams understand how to assemble, program, troubleshoot, and begin iterating on the official FRC KitBot.
* **TIMS:** Inventory and tool-management software developed for the REFINERY and made available to any FIRST team that wants to use it. *(Links to: Open TIMS · Learn More · Documentation)*
* **Event Parts Support:** Partnerships with FIRST Indiana Robotics and MentorBridge to provide live parts inventory at events, helping teams identify and access components when technical issues occur.

**Sharing What We Learn**
A shorter section covering:
* Open Alliance participation
* Public documentation
* Templates and team-development systems
* Software and digital tools
* Workshop materials
* Other open or reusable projects

## 4. News
The News section functions as both the REFINERY's public publishing platform and a curated regional robotics and STEAM news hub.

### Unified News Feed
**Combine:** REFINERY-authored posts, Team stories, Event announcements and recaps, Partnership updates, Broader FIRST community contributions, and Curated external news from local and regional sources.

**Recommended public filters:** All · REFINERY · Teams · Regional News · Partnerships · FIRST Community

### External News
The system should monitor approved regional sources through RSS or other available feeds. Potential stories enter an External News Inbox.
**An administrator can:**
* Publish, ignore, or edit metadata.
* Categorize the story and associate teams/partners/programs.
* Add an optional “Why we're sharing this” note.
* Include the story in a newsletter.

*(External stories link directly to the original publisher rather than reproducing the article.)*

### Team Associations
All news content can be associated with one or more teams. This enables:
* Filtering news by team and showing team tags on stories.
* Surfacing recent coverage within *Meet the Teams*.
* Grouping team stories in newsletters.
* Tracking media coverage internally.

### Newsletter Integration
The website and newsletter should use the same content system. Each post can be website only, included in the next newsletter, sent immediately by email, or featured on the homepage. 

The newsletter could be branded separately (e.g., *REFINERY Dispatch*) while using content published through the News system.
**A typical digest might contain:**
* From the REFINERY
* Around Northeast Indiana
* From Our Teams
* Across FIRST
* Coming Up
* How You Can Help

### Start or Host a Team
**For:** Schools, Nonprofits, Libraries, Youth organizations, Parents, and Community groups.
**Briefly explain:**
* What starting a team requires.
* What the host organization provides.
* How the REFINERY supports the process.

> **CTA:** Talk With Us About Starting a Team

### Volunteer
Allow people to express interest in:
* Technical advising & Workshop instruction
* Open-shop support & Events
* Inventory & Facility projects
* Software development & Administrative support
* Transportation & Student engagement
* Ongoing team mentoring

*(The volunteer pathway should support both one-time and long-term involvement.)*

### Partner
Describe opportunities to:
* Co-deliver programs or Provide technical expertise.
* Supply employee volunteers or Donate equipment/materials.
* Support event infrastructure or Develop shared software/systems.
* Host student career experiences or Support team launches.
* Fund regional infrastructure.

### Donate or Sponsor
Present giving around outcomes:
* Start new teams or Support team funding.
* Expand shared inventory or Purchase tools and equipment.
* Support transportation or Fund workshops.
* Support student experiences or Sustain facility operations.

## 6. About
This page establishes the REFINERY's identity, credibility, people, and regional footprint.

### Our Story
Explain:
* The challenges facing young robotics teams in Northeast Indiana.
* The origins of the shared robotics space.
* The development of the REFINERY.
* The evolution from a physical facility into a regional infrastructure organization.

### Mission and Vision
Clearly articulate the REFINERY's long-term purpose.

### How the Model Works
Use a simple visual:
> Funding + Parts + Tools + Facilities + Training + Systems + Community = **Stronger, More Sustainable Teams**

### Meet the Teams
A single directory-style section or subpage within About. *(Do not create individual team profiles.)*
**Each team card can show:**
* Team number and name (FRC or FTC)
* School or host organization and Community
* Logo or approved photo
* Short description
* Upcoming competition and Recent season highlight
* Recent associated news, where available

**Links:** Visit Team Website · TBA or FTCScout · Relevant social accounts

*(Competition information can be selectively imported from TBA, FTCScout, or the official FTC Events API. The REFINERY's own database should maintain the core team directory, while external competition data periodically enriches the cards. News stories can be associated with teams and automatically surfaced in this section.)*

### Impact and Reports
Include: Key metrics, Annual reports, Financial information, Organizational milestones, and Major funding acknowledgments.

### People and Leadership
Staff, organizational leadership, key volunteers, and appropriate governance information.

### Facility
Include: Photos, Major capabilities, Location, Parking, Accessibility, and Visitor information.

### Partners
Recognize important partners and collaborators. *(Partnerships should also be mentioned contextually elsewhere on the site when they demonstrate how the work is delivered.)*

### Contact and Policies
Include: Contact information, Youth safety, Code of conduct, Facility policies, Privacy, Financial transparency, and Other appropriate nonprofit policies.

## Connected Digital Ecosystem
The public website should be one part of a broader REFINERY digital ecosystem. All systems should share a consistent visual identity and link clearly to one another. Shared authentication can apply where appropriate.

| System | Purpose | Contents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Public Website** | Explain, communicate, demonstrate impact, and engage external audiences. | Public-facing pages detailed in this proposal. |
| **Team Portal** | Private team operations. | Funding, purchasing, team workflows, events, and internal functions. |
| **Documentation** | Detailed knowledge and reusable resources. | Technical guides, team operations, finance instructions, workshop materials, TIMS docs. |
| **TIMS** | Inventory and tool management. | Available to REFINERY teams and potentially any FIRST team that wants to use it. |
