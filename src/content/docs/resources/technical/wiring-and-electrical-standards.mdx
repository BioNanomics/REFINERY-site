These are the baseline electrical practices we follow on our own robots. They're a starting
point, not a substitute for the current season's official FIRST Robotics Competition control system documentation.

## General practices

- Follow color-coding conventions consistently across the robot so any mentor or student can
  trace a circuit at a glance.
- Label both ends of every wire run, especially anything that passes through the chassis.
- Keep wire runs away from moving mechanisms and pinch points; secure with strain relief.

## Before competition

- Re-torque all power connections (breaker, PDP/PDH, battery terminals). Vibration loosens
  hardware over a season.
- Inspect for chafed insulation anywhere a wire crosses a frame rail or moving joint.
- Confirm your wiring matches your current season's inspection checklist.
