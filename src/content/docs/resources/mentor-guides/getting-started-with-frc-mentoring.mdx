New to mentoring a FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team? This guide covers the basics of
what to expect during a build season and how to make yourself useful from day one.

## Before kickoff

- Learn the current season's game manual alongside the students — you don't need to be an
  expert on day one.
- Figure out which subteam (mechanical, electrical, programming, or business/outreach) fits
  your background best.
- Set expectations with the team's lead mentors about your availability during build season.

## During build season

- Ask questions instead of handing over answers — the goal is for students to own the
  engineering decisions.
- Keep safety front of mind, especially around power tools and pneumatics.
- Document decisions as you go; it makes the season easier to reflect on afterward.

## After the build

- Encourage students to write up what worked and what didn't for next year's team.
- Consider sharing your build openly, in the spirit of the Open Alliance — it helps the
  whole FRC community improve.
