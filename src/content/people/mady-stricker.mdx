  Mady’s involvement with FIRST began as a student on FRC Team 1501 T.H.R.U.S.T. and continued
  as a mentor for FRC Team 3494 The Quadrangles while attending Indiana University Bloomington.
  Since then, she has mentored several FIRST teams, co-founded the Indiana Scouting Alliance
  and Indiana MentorBridge, and joined the FIRST Indiana Robotics Board of Directors.

  As Operations Director of the REFINERY, Mady oversees day-to-day operations and works
  alongside its leadership and local teams to expand opportunities and build a strong,
  sustainable FIRST community in Northeast Indiana.

  Mady holds a degree in Informatics and previously worked in IT in the medical device
  manufacturing industry. Outside of FIRST, she enjoys reading, crime dramas, and spending
  time with her dog, Tank.
