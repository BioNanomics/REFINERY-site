bio: >-
  Mady's involvement with FIRST began as a high school student on FRC Team 1501, T.H.R.U.S.T.
  While attending Indiana University Bloomington, she continued in the program as a mentor
  for FRC Team 3494, The Quadrangles. Since then, she has co-founded the Indiana Scouting
  Alliance and Indiana MentorBridge and serves on the FIRST Indiana Robotics Board of
  Directors. As Operations Director of the REFINERY, Mady oversees the organization's
  day-to-day operations and works alongside its leadership and local teams to shape its
  strategic direction, develop new opportunities, and strengthen the FIRST community in
  Northeast Indiana. Mady holds a degree in Informatics from Indiana University and
  previously worked in IT in the medical device manufacturing industry. She spends much of
  her time working and volunteering within the FIRST community, but when she manages to step
  away from robots, she enjoys reading, watching crime dramas, and spending time with her
  dog, Tank.
