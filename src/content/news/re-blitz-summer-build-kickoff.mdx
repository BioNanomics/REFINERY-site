This summer, The REFINERY is running a build program centered on cloning Team 2910's
**Re•Blitz** — CAD, manufacturing, wiring, and programming, start to finish. We're documenting
the whole process openly, in the spirit of FIRST's Open Alliance, as part of what we share back
with the broader FIRST community.

Follow along on our [Projects page](../../projects/) for the full write-up, or check the linked
Chief Delphi build thread for day-to-day progress.
