Welcome to The REFINERY's new site. We're shared infrastructure for robotics teams across
Northeast Indiana: funding, parts, tools, facilities, training, and a regional community, so
that no single team or school has to build all of it alone. We also contribute back to the
wider FIRST community through projects like Robot in 3 Days, KitBot in 3 Days, and TIMS.

This site is where you'll find:

- **[What We Do](../../what-we-do/)**: the full picture of how we support teams, from
  funding to fabrication.
- **[Programs & Events](../../programs-events/)**: workshops, meetups, and public events.
- **[News](../)**: updates from The REFINERY, our teams, and the broader region.
- **[Get Involved](../../get-involved/)**: how to volunteer, partner, start a team, or give.
- **[About](../../about/)**: our story, our teams, and our people.
- **[Documentation](../../resources/)**: technical guides for mentors and teams.

We're just getting started on this new site. Check back often as we fill in the full picture
of what The REFINERY does.
