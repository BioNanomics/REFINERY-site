As part of our 2026 summer build program, The REFINERY is documenting a complete clone build
of Team 2910's **Re•Blitz** robot — from CAD review through manufacturing, wiring, and
programming — shared openly in the spirit of FIRST's Open Alliance.

## Why a clone build?

Cloning a proven competition robot is a well-established way for teams and mentors to build
real manufacturing and integration experience without the uncertainty of an original design.
It lets us focus a summer program on execution: CAD-to-part accuracy, wiring discipline, and
getting a complex mechanism running reliably.

## What we're documenting

- CAD review and any tweaks needed to match our own stock and tooling
- Manufacturing notes for each subsystem as it's built
- Wiring and electrical standup, following our own [wiring standards](../../resources/technical/wiring-and-electrical-standards/)
- Programming and initial bring-up

Follow along on the linked Chief Delphi thread for the full build log.
