Welcome to The REFINERY's new site. We're a nonprofit robotics makerspace — a shop where
students and mentors design, machine, and wire competition robots, including FIRST Robotics
Competition (FRC) builds, and a community that shares what it learns with the wider robotics
world.

This site is where you'll find:

- **[Projects](../../projects/)** — the robots and mechanisms we build, documented from
  concept to competition.
- **[Apps](../../apps/)** — software tools built by and for the makerspace, starting with
  TIMS.
- **[Blog & Resources](../)** — updates, event announcements, and technical deep-dives.
- **[Mentor Resources](../../resources/)** — practical guides for FRC mentors, drawn from our
  own experience.

We're just getting started — check back often as we document our current build and grow this
into a real resource for the FRC community.
